I'm so excited to finally share this with you

An invitation to step away

I've been working on something very special, and I'm so excited to finally share it with you.

In March 2027, I'll be hosting a Women's Wellness Retreat at the beautiful Namia River Retreat in Hoi An, Vietnam — six nights created to give you the opportunity to step away from everyday life and enjoy time dedicated to movement, relaxation, nourishing food, beautiful experiences and connection.

This isn't a retreat where every minute of your day is scheduled. There will be time to move, explore, experience Hoi An, enjoy incredible food, be pampered — and simply slow down.

And I would love you to join me.

Imagine six nights just for you

Imagine waking up in your own beautiful private pool villa surrounded by tropical greenery.

Starting your morning with breakfast and yoga. Having the freedom to explore Hoi An, enjoy wonderful food and experience Vietnamese culture.

And knowing that every day, a 90-minute Vietnamese spa treatment is waiting for you.

No rushing from one thing to the next. Just the opportunity to move, nourish, explore, connect and slow down.

Rest. Reconnect. Rejuvenate.

A peaceful island setting on the Thu Bon River.

More than a retreat

Experience Hoi An

The week isn't only about wellness inside the resort.

We'll also experience some of what makes Hoi An so special — its food, culture and beautiful riverside setting.

Together we'll enjoy a Hoi An street food experience, Merchant River Cruise and Vietnamese cooking class, along with welcome and farewell dinners.

And importantly, there will still be space in the week to simply enjoy where you are.

Thinking about coming on your own?

You absolutely can.

You don't need to come with a friend or partner to be part of this retreat.

One of the beautiful things about a small women's retreat is having the opportunity to arrive as you are, meet new people and share the experience together — while still having space for yourself.

Whether you choose twin share or your own villa, the retreat has been designed to feel welcoming, relaxed and personal.

Why I created this retreat

For me, wellness has never been about doing more.

It's about finding the things that help us feel stronger, healthier, more energised and more connected to ourselves.

I wanted to create a retreat that brought together the things at the heart of inflow-wellness — movement, nourishment and lifestyle — while also giving women something we often don't give ourselves enough of: time.

Time to slow down. Time to experience somewhere beautiful. Time to look after yourself. Time to connect. And time to have fun.

I can't wait to share this experience with the women who join me in Hoi An.
